03 · About

Led by Drew Giese, MPH

Drew Giese is a STEM Instructional Coach who has taught a variety of science courses, including AP. He is also his school's Innovation Pathways Coordinator and AP Coordinator, and serves on the MyCAP leadership team. That experience shapes one belief: great science teaching is a craft, and schools can build it on purpose.

The classroom lens sits on a lab-trained foundation: published molecular biology research, science writing for the ABC News Medical Bureau, an MPH from UMass Amherst (2003), and the pre-clinical biomedical curriculum plus clinical rotations at UMass Medical School.

Every engagement comes with two strengths: a thought partner who asks the sharp questions and stress-tests the plan, and a planning and logistics lead who handles pacing, materials, schedules, and follow-through, so every workshop and sprint runs clean.

No canned programs, no vague praise. Just concrete, usable science: lessons teachers trust, workshops they remember, programs built to last.

    06 · FAQ

    Fair questions

    How does pricing work?
    Every engagement is priced project-based, from audience, length, format, and deliverables. You get a written proposal: scope, timeline, one fixed price. Nothing metered, nothing vague.
    Virtual or in person?
    Both. Virtual work runs year-round; in-person happens during school vacations and summer, so teachers never leave classrooms for it.
    Who do you work with?
    Public school districts, charter and independent schools, nonprofits, and education companies needing STEM subject-matter expertise, from single-school projects to multi-school programs.
    What does a program review include?
    Standards-alignment review of your materials, classroom visits (virtual, or in-person during breaks and summer), teacher input, and a written, prioritized improvement roadmap.
    How far ahead should we book?
    In-person slots around school breaks and summer are limited, so briefs coming in now get first choice of the 2026–27 break calendar and summer 2027. Virtual engagements can usually start within a few weeks.
